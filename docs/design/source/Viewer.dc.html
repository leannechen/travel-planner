What a Viewer loses

Top bar: no Notes, Share, Export or collaborator avatars. Instead a “Viewing · read only” badge and Log in.
Left: Backlog only. No Archive. No Todos panel on the right.

Map View remains, with Day filter and gray Backlog markers.
Tall Event blocks show their image above the title and time, as in the editable workspace; short blocks stay text only.
Clicking an Event opens a read-only panel, same layout as the Event panel.
Export is not offered to Viewers in the PRD. Confirmed by omission, worth checking.
