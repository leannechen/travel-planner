Design notes

Landing-style page for someone who opens an invitation link while logged out. “Lena” and “Kyoto & Osaka” are sample values for the inviter’s name and the Trip title.
Two buttons, “Log in” and “Sign up”, both go to the hosted login page, on the matching tab. After login the user lands in the Trip.
The page asks for the invited email address. Logging in with another account leads to the “You don’t have access to this Trip” page from the States boards.
The header has no Log in link here because the page already has the two buttons. Language and theme switches remain.
