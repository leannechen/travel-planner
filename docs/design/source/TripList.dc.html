Notes for review

My Trips is a centered modal on desktop, opened from the Trip title (the dimmed Days stay behind it). Esc or a click outside closes it. On phone it is a full screen, reached from the Trip title (phone board #24b).
“New Trip” sits at the top of the modal. It opens the New Trip dialog with three choices: Blank Trip, Generate with AI, and Import backup….
Rows have one action: open the Trip. There is no “⋯” menu. Rename and Delete Trip live in Trip settings; Export lives in Share Trip (see the Dialogs board). To change another Trip, open it first.
“Shared with me” lists Trips where I am an Editor. Viewers with a link are not listed. Editors see Trip settings read-only (to confirm) and no Delete.
New Trip offers “Blank Trip” (title, start date, default travel mode) and “Generate with AI” (prompt field, example prompts, and the daily limit note). Each has a Back link to the chooser. While generating, see the loading state on the States board. When the daily limit is reached, the message replaces Generate (errors board).
Import backup… creates a new Trip from a full backup file. It moved here from the bottom of the old sidebar.
Delete needs its own confirmation, with a title-typing step. That step is a proposal.
Search, folders, favourites and drag-to-reorder are not in the PRD, so not drawn.
