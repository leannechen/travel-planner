Rules

A Trip may have a start date, set when creating it (Blank Trip) or in Trip settings. It is optional.
Each Day shows a date: start date plus (position − 1). Dates follow position, so reordering Days moves the contents, not the numbers or dates.
Changing the start date moves every Day’s date at once. Clearing it removes all dates. Both save at once with no confirmation.

What uses the dates

Day headers, phone Day tabs, the top-bar range, exports.
Opening-hours check: needs a start date; without one the week is shown and nothing is flagged (#44).

Ways in

Desktop: date range or “Add dates” in the top bar (proposed), or Trip list “⋯”. Phone: “…” menu. New Trip form has the same field.
Proposed here, not yet in the workspace boards: the clickable date range and the “Add dates” button, and the date picker.
