Switching Trip · what happens

1 · Pick a Trip in My Trips.

The modal closes and the Days area shows a loading skeleton (see States board).

2 · Everything switches to that Trip.

Backlog, Archive, Todos and Notes now belong to the picked Trip.

3 · Title and URL update.

The top-bar Trip title changes; the current Trip is remembered as the last opened.

4 · Unsaved edits are not lost.

Shows prompt if anything is unsaved before switching trips.

Notes for review

My Trips is a modal, not a panel: no rail, drawer or pinned column. It cost width for something used rarely, so the schedule always keeps its full width. The Trip title with its chevron is the one way in. “New Trip” sits at the top of the modal.
Opens from the Trip title or a shortcut. Shortcuts are decided later, together with all the others.
Phone: the same list is a full screen, opened from the Trip title (Trip list board).
Backlog and Archive collapse separately (frame C), each with its own arrow. When both are collapsed the column becomes a narrow strip with their counts (frame D); a click on an icon reopens that panel. The strip is the left edge now that the rail is gone. Dropping an Event on a strip files it there.
