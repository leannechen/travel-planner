Design notes

While a guest is not logged in, the top bar shows one primary button, “Log in to save your progress”, where the account avatar would be. On phone it is a slim banner under the top bar and also the first row of the “…” menu.
The button and the banner go straight to the hosted login page, with no dialog in between. The login screens themselves are not drawn here.
Share, Export, Language and Theme sit at the bottom of the left sidebar on desktop (same as logged-in users), and are rows in the “…” menu on phone. They are the same for guests and logged-in users.
The menu’s first row is guest-only. Logged-in users see Export…, Trip settings…, Language and Theme.
