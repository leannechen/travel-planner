Design notes

A Leg exists only after the user chooses Add Travel in the gap between two adjacent Events; the system never adds one. The buttons appear on hover or tap of the empty slot (see the Add Event / Add Travel board on Page 2). The chooser in frame 1 opens with the Trip’s default mode already selected; OK creates the Leg, Cancel or Esc leaves the gap empty.
The Leg popover opens to the right of its chip and sits above everything in the Day column and the next column, including the chip it belongs to. The chip stays highlighted while the popover is open.
Duration and distance are computed and read-only. Gap is shown quietly, and in amber only when shorter than the trip. No notes field and no transit lines or transfers, per the Leg spec. “Open in Google Maps” is a link out.
Duration comes from the two locations and the mode. Changing mode or a location recalculates it. A Leg overrides the Trip default per Leg.
If the Events stop being adjacent or leave the Day, the Leg is removed (no UI for that).
A Lodging can start the Leg to the first Event of the Day (see the earlier Lodging board). Not drawn as a separate state here.
Warning shows when the gap is shorter than the duration. Nothing moves.
Whether “Delete Leg” asks to confirm is open. In the cannot-calculate state duration and distance show a dash, there is no Maps link, and no “Add a location” button since either or both Events may lack a location.
