Design notes

An empty time slot between two Events shows its buttons on hover (desktop) or when tapped (phone). Nothing is shown at rest.
Both neighbouring Events have a location set from Google: the slot offers “Add Event” and “Add Travel”.
Otherwise it offers “Add Event” only: a neighbour has no location, or only a free-text place, or the slot is before the first Event or after the last one. A Day’s Lodging at the top counts as a neighbour for the first slot.
“Add Event” opens a new Event in that slot. “Add Travel” opens the travel-mode chooser, with the Trip’s default mode selected (see the Leg board).
Where a Leg already exists, the slot shows the Leg chip and not these buttons.
The buttons sit in a small floating group centred on the slot, so they fit even when the gap is short; they appear above the neighbouring cards. The slot is outlined with a dashed highlight.
On phone the buttons are 44 px high. Tapping outside, or the highlighted slot again, dismisses them.
