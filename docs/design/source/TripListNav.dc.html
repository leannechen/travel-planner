Switching Trip · what happens

1 · Pick a Trip in the drawer.

The row highlights; Days area shows a loading skeleton (see States board).

2 · Drawer closes if it was a drawer.

If pinned it stays open. Backlog, Archive, Todos and Notes now belong to the picked Trip.

3 · Title and URL update.

The top-bar Trip title changes; the current Trip is remembered as the last opened.

4 · Unsaved edits are not lost.

Shows prompt if anything is unsaved before switching trips.

Notes for review

No rail: it cost width for something used rarely and did the same job as the Trip title, so it was dropped. The Trip title with its chevron is the one way in. “New Trip” lives at the top of the list.
A is the default, so the schedule keeps its full width. B opens as a drawer over the Backlog, so nothing shifts. C is for people who switch often; pinning is remembered per user.
Opens from the Trip title or a shortcut. Shortcuts are decided later, together with all the others.
Phone: no rail. The Trips screen opens from the Trip title (earlier board).
Backlog and Archive collapse separately (frame D), each with its own arrow. When both are collapsed the column becomes a narrow strip with their counts (frame E); a click on an icon reopens that panel. The strip is the left edge now that the rail is gone. Dropping an Event on a strip files it there.
