Warning

One general text, “Opening hours may conflict”: amber label and border on the card, the same text beside the hours in the panel (so it shows on phone too).
No detail, no toast, no dismiss. It goes by itself when the problem goes. “Remove opening hours” is the way out.
Events in the Backlog or Archive, and Trips without a start date, show no label.
