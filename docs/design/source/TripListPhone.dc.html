Notes for review

Phone version of the My Trips changes. My Trips is a full screen from the Trip title; its “New Trip” button opens the same three choices as desktop, as bottom sheets: Blank Trip, Generate with AI, Import backup….
Rows have one action: open the Trip. No “⋯” menu. Rename and Delete Trip are in Trip settings (frames 5 and 6); backup is in Share Trip (frame 7).
Blank Trip and Generate with AI each have a Back button to the chooser. Create Trip and Generate Trip are the full-width main actions. See the States board for loading and the daily limit.
On phone the top-bar Share icon opens Share Trip, and the “…” menu opens Trip settings. The Export entry in that menu is gone (Phone menus board still shows it).
Delete Trip keeps the typed-title confirmation. Buttons are stacked and full width for thumbs.
