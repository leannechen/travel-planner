Behaviour

Opens with the shortcut or the Notes button, from Schedule or Map. The new-Note field is focused.
The panel floats above both views. Drag the header to place it anywhere on screen; it keeps within the window.
Open or closed, and position, are kept when switching Schedule and Map, and remembered when the panel is closed and reopened.
Order: new field, pinned Notes, then the rest by most recent edit. Edit and Preview tabs for Markdown. No search in v1.
Notes autosave like Event fields. Esc closes the panel.
Viewers have no Notes button (see Viewer board).

For review

Shortcut Ctrl ⇧ N is a placeholder. PRD says app-wide actions use modifier combinations; the earlier workspace board shows a bare N, which should be aligned.
On phone, Notes would open as a full-height sheet since a floating panel cannot sit anywhere. Not drawn yet.
Whether position is remembered across sessions is a proposal.
