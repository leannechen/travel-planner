Design notes

The top right reads, left to right: Notes, “?”, language, theme, then the account spot. Logged out, that spot is a “Log in” button; logged in, it is a round avatar with the first letter of the email.
The menu has the email, “My Trips” and “Log out”, and nothing else. There is no profile or preferences screen. “My Trips” opens the Trip list; “Log out” ends the session at once and shows the Landing page (next board).
Language and theme stay as their own switches next to the avatar, not inside the menu. Menu rows are 34 px on desktop and 44 px on phone.
In the guest workspace the logged-out button keeps its longer label, “Log in to save your progress”. Elsewhere, such as the session-expired banner, it is the plain “Log in”.
The avatar shows a letter because Auth0 gives us an email and not necessarily a name or a picture.
