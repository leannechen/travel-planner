Notes for review

Both are centered dialogs with a dimmed backdrop. Overlay click or Esc closes. Share Trip has no Done button: its switch and invites apply at once. Trip settings has Cancel and Save: nothing changes until Save (the X or Cancel discards edits). Phone versions are bottom sheets (My Trips phone board).
Export is only Full backup now. Document and Image export are removed. Backup stays available when the link is off, and a backup is restored from New Trip › Import backup….
People list: an avatar with the initial and the email, with the role on the right (Owner, Editor). A pending invite shows an envelope avatar, “Invited”, a copy-invite-link button and a remove button.
Link sharing and Invite are Owner-only. Editors would open Share Trip and see only Export (to confirm).
Trip settings holds Rename (the Trip title field) and Delete Trip…, moved here from the old Trip row menu. Delete has its own confirmation (Trip list board).
Trip settings has no language selector. Language and theme live only in the top right of the top bar (phone: the “…” menu).
